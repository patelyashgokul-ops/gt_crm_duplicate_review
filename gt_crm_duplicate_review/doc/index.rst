CRM Duplicate Review
====================

CRM Duplicate Review helps CRM teams identify and review possible duplicate
leads and opportunities before invoking Odoo's native CRM merge.

The customer guide is included in ``documentation/USER_GUIDE.md``.

The product operates on ``crm.lead`` only. It does not merge automatically,
does not deduplicate contacts, and does not reconcile accounting or sales
documents. Similarity scores are heuristic rankings, not probabilities.
