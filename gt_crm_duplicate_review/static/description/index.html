MATCHING INTELLIGENCE

Clear signals, explainable scores.

Matching uses normalized CRM data and deterministic heuristic scores. Scores rank evidence; they are not probabilities or proof that two opportunities are the same business deal.

  • Exact normalized email and valid phone/mobile matches.
  • Phone-to-mobile comparisons when the normalized numbers are equivalent.
  • Conservative near-email matching; near-phone matching is disabled where safe equivalence cannot be established.
  • Generic mailboxes and shared contact details are cautions, not an unsafe universal blacklist.
  • Invalid and blank identifiers do not create matches.

SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISON

Compare CRM records before deciding.

Review lead type, contact, customer, email, phone, mobile, salesperson, sales team, stage, expected revenue, probability, priority and record status. Conflict indicators are informational and never reconcile CRM fields automatically.

Illustrative example: Two opportunities share a valid email and phone, producing a 95/100 heuristic score. Their names and pipeline stages differ, and the review shows those conflicts. If they represent separate product projects, the manager can record “Separate sales opportunities”; neither source record is changed by that decision.

CONTROLLED WORKFLOW

Review → Approve → Confirm → Native CRM Merge.

The manager reviews the pair, approves it with a reason, then separately acknowledges the irreversible native CRM merge. The server revalidates access, company, source freshness and approval before it runs the native behavior.

GETTING STARTED

Set up your review team.

Dependency: Odoo Community CRM (`crm`).

Roles: Duplicate Review User and Duplicate Review Manager.

Scanning: Manual company-scoped scan; scheduled scanning is disabled by default and configurable by an authorized administrator.

Read the customer guide

  1. Install the module. Add `gt_crm_duplicate_review` to an Odoo addons path, update Apps, and install CRM Duplicate Review.
  2. Assign roles. Give sales users Duplicate Review User; assign Duplicate Review Manager only to reviewers who need scan and decision actions.
  3. Open CRM → Duplicate Review. Use Dashboard or Review Queue. Company-less leads are excluded from automatic pairing.
  4. Run a bounded scan. A manager scans the authorized company. Continue partial scans as indicated in Scan History.
  5. Review before merging. Inspect source permissions, conflicts and freshness. Not Duplicate records remain unchanged; merges require approval and a separate explicit confirmation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Does a high score prove the opportunities are duplicates?

No. It is a heuristic ranking of match evidence. Review the commercial requirement and conflicts before deciding.

Does the module merge automatically?

No. An authorized manager must review, approve and separately confirm the native CRM merge.

Can I mark two shared-contact opportunities as separate?

Yes. A manager can record Not Duplicate with a reason. The source CRM records are not modified by that decision.

What if a source record changes after approval?

The approval becomes stale and the merge is blocked until the evidence is rechecked and approved again.

Does this merge contacts or sales documents?

No. The module reviews `crm.lead` leads and opportunities only; contact deduplication and accounting or sales-document reconciliation are outside scope.

Does it grant users additional CRM access?

No. Case visibility and actions continue to depend on access to both underlying CRM records and the company.
