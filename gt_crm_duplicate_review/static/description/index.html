INSTALLATION & CONFIGURATION

Set up your review team.

Odoo 20 Community
CRM

Open the customer guide

  1. Install the add-on. Place gt_crm_duplicate_review on your addons path, restart Odoo, update the Apps list and install CRM Duplicate Review. The module depends on Community CRM.
  2. Assign review roles. Assign Duplicate Review User to permitted reviewers and Duplicate Review Manager only to users who need scan and decision actions. These groups do not grant extra CRM record access.
  3. Open the review workspace. Go to CRM → Duplicate Review → Dashboard or Review Queue. Confirm users can access both source CRM records and the company.
  4. Run a bounded scan. A manager scans one authorized company. If the scan reports partial progress, run it again to continue; company-less leads are not paired automatically.
  5. Review and resolve cases. Compare both opportunities. Mark Not Duplicate with a reason or approve a merge; final native merging requires a separate explicit confirmation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know.

What does a match score mean?

It is a deterministic heuristic ranking of matching evidence, not a probability that records are the same customer or the same deal.

Does the module merge records automatically?

No. Matching only creates review suggestions. Managers must review, approve and separately confirm the native CRM merge.

Can two opportunities share a contact and still be distinct?

Yes. Separate projects, renewals or products may legitimately share customer and contact information. Review the opportunity details and use Not Duplicate with a reason when appropriate.

What happens when source CRM data changes after approval?

The approval is marked stale and execution is blocked until evidence is rechecked and the case is approved again.

Does a score of 95 mean a 95% chance?

No. Scores are fixed heuristic rankings; they are not calibrated probabilities and never authorize a merge by themselves.

Can users see CRM records they could not access before?

No. Case searches, comparisons and dashboard counts are restricted by access to both underlying CRM records and the applicable company.

Can the module merge contacts or sales documents?

No. The module reviews crm.lead records only; contact deduplication and accounting or sales-document reconciliation are outside scope.
